Privacy Policy - Shorts SK Uploader

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Shorts SK Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel "준&하린의 직장인 영어" (https://www.youtube.com/@bizeng4sk) to upload the channel's own videos, post and read comments on the channel's own videos, and read the channel's own analytics.

1. Who uses this app

This app is used only by the channel owner. It is not offered to the public, and no other users can sign in.

2. What data the app accesses

With the owner's permission through Google OAuth, the app accesses the owner's YouTube channel only: uploading videos, managing video titles and descriptions, posting replies to comments, reading comments, and reading channel analytics (views, watch time).

3. How the data is used

The data is used only to publish and manage the owner's own videos and to measure their performance. The data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

4. How the data is stored

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer and in the owner's own Google Apps Script project. They are never published or shared.

5. Removing access

The owner can remove this app's access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

6. Use of Google user data

This app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy and the YouTube API Services Terms of Service (https://www.youtube.com/t/terms). Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

7. Contact

Please contact the channel owner through the YouTube channel above.
